Ultimate Financial Calculators Guide (India)

Plan investments, loans, savings and taxes smarter — Updated for 2026

Whether you are investing, taking a loan, saving for the future, or filing taxes, financial calculators help you make confident decisions based on real numbers instead of guesswork.

📈 SIP Calculator — The Wealth Builder

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) allows you to invest a fixed amount every month into mutual funds. Over time, the power of compounding can turn small investments into substantial wealth.

Example: Investing ₹5,000/month for 20 years at 12% annual return can grow to more than ₹50 lakhs.

  • ✔ Ideal for long-term goals
  • ✔ Reduces market timing risk
  • ✔ Suitable for beginners
🚀 Calculate SIP Returns

🏦 EMI Calculator — Plan Your Loan Smartly

Before taking any loan — home, car or personal — you must know your monthly EMI. An EMI calculator helps you estimate payments based on loan amount, interest rate and tenure.

Example: ₹10 lakh loan at 9% for 5 years → EMI ≈ ₹20,758/month.

  • ✔ Compare loan options instantly
  • ✔ Choose affordable tenure
  • ✔ Avoid financial stress
💳 Calculate EMI

🏠 Home Loan Eligibility Calculator

Banks do not approve loans based only on salary. They evaluate your repayment capacity using factors like income, existing EMIs, interest rate and tenure.

Knowing your eligibility helps you choose properties within budget and avoid loan rejection.

  • ✔ Based on FOIR (income vs obligations)
  • ✔ Estimates maximum loan amount
  • ✔ Shows required down payment
🏠 Check Eligibility

🧾 Income Tax Calculator — Old vs New Regime

India currently offers two tax systems. The old regime allows deductions while the new regime offers lower tax rates with fewer deductions.

Choosing the correct regime can save thousands of rupees annually.

  • ✔ Compare both regimes instantly
  • ✔ Includes standard deduction benefits
  • ✔ Useful for salaried individuals
🧮 Calculate Income Tax

🧮 GST Calculator — Instant Tax Calculation

GST is applied to most goods and services in India. This calculator helps you add or remove GST from prices at common rates like 5%, 12%, 18% and 28%.

  • ✔ Useful for businesses and freelancers
  • ✔ Calculate inclusive or exclusive price
  • ✔ Ideal for invoices and billing
📊 Calculate GST

🏦 FD Calculator — Safe Investment Planning

Fixed Deposits are among the safest investment options in India. Use this calculator to estimate maturity value and interest earnings.

  • ✔ Low risk investment
  • ✔ Predictable returns
  • ✔ Ideal for conservative investors
🏦 Calculate FD Returns

📊 CAGR Calculator — True Growth Rate

CAGR shows the actual yearly growth of an investment, smoothing out market fluctuations. It helps compare different investment options fairly.

  • ✔ Useful for stocks and mutual funds
  • ✔ Measures long-term performance
  • ✔ Removes volatility effects
📈 Calculate CAGR
