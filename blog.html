📈 SIP Calculator — The Wealth Builder

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) allows you to invest a fixed amount every month into mutual funds and benefit from long-term compounding.

💡 Why SIP Works:
• Power of compounding
• Rupee cost averaging
• Disciplined long-term investing

Example: ₹5,000/month for 10 years at 12% → ~₹11–12 lakhs.
