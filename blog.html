Ultimate Financial Calculators Guide (India)

Plan investments, loans, savings and taxes smarter — Updated for 2026

Money decisions today are rarely simple. Whether you are investing for the future, planning a major purchase, or managing taxes, even small mistakes can cost thousands of rupees over time. Financial calculators remove the guesswork and replace assumptions with clear numbers, helping you plan with confidence instead of uncertainty.

📈 SIP Calculator — Building Wealth Month by Month

A Systematic Investment Plan allows you to invest a fixed amount regularly into mutual funds instead of waiting to accumulate a large sum. This approach is popular in India because it makes long-term investing accessible even with modest monthly savings.

Over long periods, compounding does most of the heavy lifting. For instance, a monthly investment of ₹5,000 at an average annual return of 12% can grow into several lakhs over two decades. What matters most is consistency rather than timing the market perfectly.

🚀 Calculate SIP Returns

🏦 EMI Calculator — Know the Real Cost of a Loan

Loans make big purchases possible, but the monthly repayment determines how comfortable or stressful that decision becomes. An EMI calculator shows your exact monthly obligation based on the loan amount, interest rate and tenure, so there are no surprises later.

For example, a ₹10 lakh loan at 9% interest over five years results in a monthly payment of roughly ₹20,758. Even a small change in tenure or rate can significantly alter this number, which is why checking different scenarios before signing a loan agreement is essential.

💳 Calculate EMI

🏠 Home Loan Eligibility — How Much House Can You Afford?

Banks do not simply approve loans based on salary alone. They evaluate your overall repayment capacity by looking at income, existing obligations, interest rates and loan duration. Understanding your eligibility beforehand helps you search for properties within a realistic budget.

This also reduces the chances of rejection and gives you clarity on the down payment you may need. In competitive real estate markets, being financially prepared can make a significant difference.

🏠 Check Eligibility

🧾 Income Tax Calculator — Choosing the Right Regime

India currently offers two tax systems, each suited to different financial situations. The old regime allows multiple deductions for investments and expenses, while the new regime offers lower tax rates but fewer deductions.

Selecting the wrong option can mean paying more tax than necessary. Comparing both systems using actual income figures helps you identify which one saves more money in your specific case rather than relying on general advice.

🧮 Calculate Income Tax

🧮 GST Calculator — Quick Pricing and Billing Support

Goods and Services Tax applies to most products and services in India, making it a routine part of business transactions as well as everyday purchases. Whether you are preparing invoices, setting product prices, or simply understanding a bill, calculating GST correctly is important.

This tool helps you instantly add or remove GST at standard rates such as 5%, 12%, 18% and 28%, making it useful for freelancers, shop owners, students and salaried individuals alike.

📊 Calculate GST

🏦 FD Calculator — Predictable Returns with Low Risk

Fixed Deposits remain one of the most trusted investment options in India, especially for people who prefer stability over market-linked fluctuations. They offer guaranteed returns and are commonly used for short- to medium-term goals.

Knowing the maturity value in advance helps you decide how much to invest and for how long, whether you are saving for an emergency fund, a major purchase, or retirement income.

🏦 Calculate FD Returns

📊 CAGR Calculator — Understanding Real Growth

Investment returns rarely move in a straight line. CAGR, or Compound Annual Growth Rate, converts uneven growth into a single annual figure, making it easier to compare different investments objectively.

This metric is widely used to evaluate stocks, mutual funds, business growth and long-term performance trends. By focusing on the overall growth rate instead of short-term fluctuations, it gives a clearer picture of progress over time.

📈 Calculate CAGR

🔢 Percentage Calculator — Useful in Everyday Decisions

Percentages appear in countless situations, from shopping discounts and exam results to profit margins and tax calculations. Even simple percentage errors can lead to incorrect conclusions when dealing with money or performance data.

Having a quick way to calculate increases, decreases or portions makes daily tasks easier and reduces the chance of manual mistakes.

🔢 Calculate Percentage

🎂 Age Calculator — Exact Age Without Manual Counting

Determining exact age can be surprisingly tricky, especially when forms or legal requirements demand precision down to the day. This tool calculates your age in years, months and days instantly based on your date of birth.

It is commonly used for official documentation, insurance applications, school admissions and competitive exams where eligibility depends on specific age limits.

🎂 Calculate Age
