📈 SIP Calculator — The Wealth Builder

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is the most popular investment method among Indian salaried individuals. Instead of investing a lump sum, you invest a fixed amount monthly into mutual funds.

💡 Why SIP Works:
• Power of compounding
• Rupee cost averaging
• Disciplined investing habit

Example: ₹5,000/month for 10 years at 12% can grow to approximately ₹11–12 lakhs.
