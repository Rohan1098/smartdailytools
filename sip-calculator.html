About SIP Calculator

SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) is one of the most popular ways to build long-term wealth through mutual funds in India. By investing a fixed amount every month, investors benefit from rupee cost averaging and the power of compounding.

Our free SIP calculator helps you estimate the future value of your monthly investments based on expected returns and tenure. It is useful for retirement planning, wealth creation goals and disciplined investing.

📊 Example

If you invest ₹5,000/month for 10 years at 12% return, your investment can grow significantly due to compounding.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good SIP return in India?

Historically, equity mutual funds in India have delivered around 10–14% annual returns over the long term, though actual returns vary.

Is SIP better than lump sum investment?

SIP helps average market volatility through rupee cost averaging, making it suitable for most retail investors.
