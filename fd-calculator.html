About FD Calculator

A Fixed Deposit (FD) is one of the safest investment options in India. This calculator helps you estimate the maturity amount based on investment, interest rate, tenure, and compounding frequency.

How FD Interest is Calculated

FD returns are calculated using compound interest. More frequent compounding (like quarterly or monthly) results in slightly higher returns.
