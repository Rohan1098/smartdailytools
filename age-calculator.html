About This Age Calculator

This free Age Calculator instantly calculates your age using your date of birth. It provides an accurate breakdown of your age in years, months, and days.

The calculator also shows the total number of days you have lived and calculates the remaining time until your next birthday.

Many people use age calculators while filling government forms, job applications, insurance forms, exam registrations, and official documents.
