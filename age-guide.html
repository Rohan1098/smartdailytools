How Age is Calculated

Age is calculated by subtracting your date of birth from the current date. However, because months have different lengths and leap years exist, accurate calculation requires careful adjustment of years, months, and days.

For example, someone born on 15 March 2000 will not turn 25 until 15 March 2025 — even though the year difference becomes 25 earlier.

Age in Years, Months, and Days

A precise age calculation usually provides three values:

  • Years — Complete years lived
  • Months — Remaining months after last birthday
  • Days — Remaining days after last full month

This detailed breakdown is commonly required in government documents and medical records.

Why Age Calculation Can Be Tricky

Manual age calculation can be confusing because:

  • Months have 28 to 31 days
  • Leap years add an extra day in February
  • Different countries use different date formats
  • Partial years must be handled correctly

An online calculator eliminates these issues and provides instant accurate results.

Common Uses of Age Calculators

Age calculators are widely used for:

  • School and college admissions
  • Government exams and job eligibility
  • Retirement planning
  • Insurance policies
  • Medical records
  • Personal milestones
