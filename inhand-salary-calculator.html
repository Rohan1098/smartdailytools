About This Calculator

This Income Tax Calculator helps Indian taxpayers estimate their tax liability and monthly in-hand salary using the latest tax slabs for FY 2025-26 (Assessment Year 2026-27).

It supports both the New Tax Regime and Old Tax Regime with optional investment deductions.

Latest Tax Slabs FY 2025-26

New Tax Regime

  • ₹0 – ₹4,00,000 : 0%
  • ₹4,00,000 – ₹8,00,000 : 5%
  • ₹8,00,000 – ₹12,00,000 : 10%
  • ₹12,00,000 – ₹16,00,000 : 15%
  • ₹16,00,000 – ₹20,00,000 : 20%
  • ₹20,00,000 – ₹24,00,000 : 25%
  • Above ₹24,00,000 : 30%

Old Tax Regime

  • ₹0 – ₹2,50,000 : 0%
  • ₹2,50,000 – ₹5,00,000 : 5%
  • ₹5,00,000 – ₹10,00,000 : 20%
  • Above ₹10,00,000 : 30%
