EMI Calculator India — Complete Guide (2026)

Understand EMI before taking any loan

🏦 What is EMI?

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is the fixed monthly payment you make to repay a loan. It includes both principal and interest components.

🧮 EMI Formula

EMI Formula:

EMI = P × r × (1+r)n / ((1+r)n − 1)
  • P = Loan amount
  • r = Monthly interest rate
  • n = Number of months

📊 Example (India 2026)

Loan Rate Tenure EMI
₹10,00,000 9% 5 years ≈ ₹20,758

💡 Pro Tips

  • Keep EMI ≤ 30% of salary
  • Shorter tenure = less interest
  • Compare interest rates before loan
